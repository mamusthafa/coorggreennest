Why Stay Here

A private homestay close to Madikeri town

Coorg Green Nest is designed for guests who want the warmth of a homestay with the convenience of a villa. You are close enough to Madikeri for sightseeing, shopping and food, while still enjoying a quiet residential setting.

  • Spacious 7 BHK villa suitable for families, couples and groups.
  • Attached bathrooms in every room for comfort and privacy.
  • 24-hour hot water and dedicated parking for a relaxed stay.
  • Only 2.5 km from Madikeri town and well placed for major sightseeing spots.
